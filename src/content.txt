Welcome to Rhynnies Stars Football Academy
Rhynnies Stars Football Academy is a community-based youth football development initiative affiliated with Van Rhyn Primary School in Windhoek, Namibia.
Our mission is to provide structured football development opportunities that promote sporting excellence, education, discipline, leadership, and community engagement.
Through football, mentorship, and positive role models, we help young people develop the skills, confidence, character, and resilience needed to succeed both on and off the field.

ABOUT US
Our Story
Rhynnies Stars Football Academy was founded from the football programme of Van Rhyn Primary School in Windhoek, Namibia.
Over time, teachers, coaches, parents, and community members recognised the need for a football development structure that could serve both learners from the school and talented young footballers from the wider community.
The Academy has since evolved into a community-focused development programme dedicated to nurturing football talent while promoting education, discipline, leadership, and positive social values.
We believe that no child should be denied the opportunity to participate in organised sport because of financial circumstances.

Vision

COMPETITIONS
Competitive Football Pathway
Rhynnies Stars Football Academy provides players with opportunities to participate in organised football competitions that support both player development and competitive growth.

Khomas Schools Sport Region (KSSR) League
Through our affiliation with Van Rhyn Primary School, players participate in regional school football competitions that promote sporting excellence, teamwork, and school pride.

MTC HopSol Youth Soccer League
The Academy also participates in the MTC HopSol Youth Soccer League, one of Namibia's most recognised youth football development competitions.
Participation in the league provides players with valuable competitive experience and exposure to structured football development opportunities.
